# Aesthetic Families

A starting vocabulary for the `DESIGN.md`'s "aesthetic family" field — pick one and commit
to it, rather than blending several into something incoherent. These are generic
categories, not any specific product's system; treat them as a naming convenience, not a
template to fill in mechanically.

- **Editorial minimalism** — large type, generous whitespace, restrained palette, content
  carries the hierarchy instead of decoration.
- **Terminal / technical** — monospace accents, high contrast, grid-aligned, precise,
  low ornament.
- **Warm / tactile** — natural materials and color temperature, soft light, texture used
  deliberately.
- **Data-dense** — information-forward, smaller type at higher density, utilitarian
  chrome, built for scanning over browsing.
- **Cinematic** — deep shadow, one controlled light source or accent, deliberate pacing
  even in a static UI.
- **Playful** — rounded forms, higher color saturation, motion used for delight rather
  than function alone.
- **Glass / translucent** — layered transparency and blur used sparingly for depth, not
  as decoration on every surface.
- **Brutalist** — raw structure exposed, minimal ornament, deliberate rawness prioritized
  over polish.
- **Indie / handcrafted** — asymmetry, imperfection, and personality prioritized over a
  systematized grid.

Naming the family in `DESIGN.md` and referring back to it when reviewing new UI work makes
drift easier to catch — "this button doesn't fit editorial minimalism" is a checkable
statement; "this doesn't feel right" is not.
